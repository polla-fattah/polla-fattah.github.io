<p>Kurdish Automatic Text Summarization focuses on developing extractive and abstractive summarization frameworks capable of condensing lengthy Kurdish documents into concise, informative summaries while preserving semantic integrity.</p>

<h2 class="section-heading">Research Approach</h2>

<p>The research investigates transformer-based language representations, including Kurdish-adapted BERT and sequence-to-sequence models, to handle Kurdish sentence structures and generate coherent summaries.</p>

<h3>Key Features</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Extractive summarization using neural sentence ranking</li>
  <li>Abstractive summarization using encoder-decoder transformers</li>
  <li>Handling multi-document Kurdish news and academic articles</li>
  <li>Automated evaluation using Kurdish-adapted ROUGE metrics</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="section-heading">Impact</h2>

<p>Automatic summarization enables efficient information assimilation across Kurdish news aggregators, research repositories, and digital archives.</p>
