<p>This initiative focuses on establishing digital platforms and computational frameworks for collaborative Kurdish dictionary construction, lexical verification, and multidisciplinary terminology management across dialects.</p>

<h2 class="section-heading">Research Scope</h2>

<p>Developed in collaboration with academic and linguistic institutions, the project bridges traditional lexicography with modern database technologies to preserve and expand Kurdish vocabulary in technical and academic spheres.</p>

<h3>Key Focus Areas</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Collaborative digital dictionary and lexicon platforms</li>
  <li>Lexical verification workflows and peer-review mechanisms</li>
  <li>Multi-dialect lexical correspondence and etymology mapping</li>
  <li>Crowdsourced expert contributions and consensus tracking</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="section-heading">Impact</h2>

<p>By providing structured, searchable, and verified digital lexical resources, this work supports education, publishing, and language technologies across Kurdistan.</p>
