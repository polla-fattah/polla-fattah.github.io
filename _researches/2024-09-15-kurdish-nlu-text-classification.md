<p>Kurdish Natural Language Understanding (NLU) and Text Classification investigates deep learning and contextual representation models designed to understand semantics, categorize documents, and extract information from Kurdish texts.</p>

<h2 class="section-heading">Research Objectives</h2>

<p>The research explores adapting pretrained language models and transformer architectures to Kurdish linguistic structures, evaluating semantic comprehension across diverse domains and genres.</p>

<h3>Key Areas</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Multi-class document and topic classification</li>
  <li>Named Entity Recognition (NER) and semantic role labeling</li>
  <li>Intent recognition and contextual semantic understanding</li>
  <li>Evaluating fine-tuned transformer models for Kurdish</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="section-heading">Applications</h2>

<p>This work provides foundational tools for automated content indexing, digital archiving, legal and academic document organization, and smart information retrieval in Kurdish.</p>
