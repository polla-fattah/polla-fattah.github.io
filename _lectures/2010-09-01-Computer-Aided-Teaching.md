## Overview

In 2010, this professional development training was delivered to university educators and lecturers at Salahaddin University-Erbil as part of the pedagogical training programme. The course focused on empowering academic staff with modern computer-aided teaching (CAT) methodologies and integrating educational technologies into curriculum delivery.

## Aims

- Introduce educators to modern computer-aided instruction and digital teaching methodologies
- Demonstrate the effective use of educational software, multimedia, and digital tools in classroom pedagogy
- Provide hands-on training for designing interactive lectures and digital course materials
- Equip teachers with effective digital assessment and student engagement techniques

## Audience

University lecturers, educators, and newly appointed faculty members across diverse academic departments participating in pedagogical certification and academic training.

## Outcomes

Participants developed practical competency in leveraging computer-based tools and digital presentation strategies to enhance pedagogical efficacy, modernizing course delivery across departments at Salahaddin University-Erbil.
