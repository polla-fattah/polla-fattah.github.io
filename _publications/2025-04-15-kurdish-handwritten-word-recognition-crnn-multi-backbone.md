  This study explores offline handwritten word recognition for Central Kurdish (Sorani) script using a Convolutional Recurrent Neural Network (CRNN) framework with Connectionist Temporal Classification (CTC). The research presents an empirical multi-backbone evaluation across various CNN feature extraction architectures, analyzing recognition accuracy, character error rates, and sequence modeling capabilities for low-resource Kurdish handwriting.
