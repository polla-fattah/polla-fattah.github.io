## Research Focus

Fanar's PhD research focused on sentiment analysis for Kurdish, particularly Central Kurdish (Sorani) social media texts. The work examines lexicon-based, machine learning, and deep learning approaches for a low-resource language, including dialectal variation, dual script systems, data scarcity, and informal short-text preprocessing.

## Research Outcomes

- Completed the PhD and successfully defended the thesis in a viva
- Co-authored a comprehensive review of sentiment analysis approaches and challenges in Kurdish social media texts, presented at the International Conference on Breakthroughs in Artificial Intelligence
